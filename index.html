Khushali Jaiswal
Fashion & Graphic Designer

A fashion & graphic designer and curator of elegance, form, and visual storytelling, Khushali brings technical skill and creativity together to create distinctive, trend-aware visual experiences.

Driven by curiosity and a constant desire to explore, she enjoys pushing beyond familiar boundaries and taking on new challenges that expand both her skills and perspective.

Her design philosophy is rooted in the belief that fashion is a powerful medium for individuals to articulate their distinct identities and lifestyles through what they wear. That principle informs her approach across fashion, graphic design, illustration, branding, publication and visual communication.

She is passionate about personal and professional growth, and about creating work that feels considered, expressive and memorable.
